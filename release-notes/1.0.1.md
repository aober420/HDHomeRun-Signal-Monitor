## ✨ New Features

- **Channel-scan status:** See scan progress and the number of channels found while an HDHomeRun channel scan is running.
- **Tuned frequency:** Show the tuner's current frequency when it has not reported a channel number.

## 🐛 Bug Fixes

- **Hidden scan readings:** Resolved an issue where signal readings during channel scans were incorrectly hidden as idle.
- **Scan warnings:** Avoid showing antenna-adjustment warnings while a scan is testing frequencies without a selected channel.

## ⚙️ Improvements

- **Missing measurements:** Clearly distinguish idle tuners from scans where the device is not supplying reception measurements. Older devices without scan-status support continue to show their available tuner readings.
